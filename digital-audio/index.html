DigitalAudio

Everything that hits your ears is air being shoved about. This subject follows that shove all the way in — through a microphone, into 44,100 numbers a second, through filters and envelopes — and all the way back out of a speaker.

When sampling lies

Sample too slowly and you don't just lose a frequency — you record a different one that was never there. The Nyquist limit, and the wheels that spin backwards.

Filters are made of arithmetic

Noise-cancelling headphones, tone controls and reverb are all the same thing — adding and averaging numbers in a list, fast enough that you hear the result.
