Making sound move

Why a held note wobbles when a singer holds it, and why a siren rises and falls — one signal quietly steering another.

Before you start

Oscillator
the part that makes the wobble in the first place. It is the source of the sound — everything else in a synth only shapes what the oscillator produces.
Cutoff
the frequency where a filter starts removing things. Set the cutoff low and only the deepest parts survive; raise it and the brightness comes back.
Pitch
how high or low a note sounds. It is what your ear makes of frequency, so a 400 Hz wobble is a lower pitch than an 800 Hz one.
Hz = hertz
cycles per second — how many complete wobbles fit into one second. 440 Hz means 440 every second, which is the A that an orchestra tunes to.

Where you meet this every day

Listen to a singer hold a long note. The pitch doesn’t sit perfectly still — it wavers gently up and down, a few times a second. That’s vibrato, and it’s most of why a held human note sounds alive instead of like a test tone. A siren does the louder version of the same thing: pitch sweeping up, then down, over and over.

One signal steering another

That’s modulation: one signal controlling something about another. The controller here is an LFO — a low frequency oscillator. It’s an ordinary oscillator, just running far too slowly to hear as a note. Five wobbles per second is below the range your ears read as pitch, so you don’t hear the LFO itself. You hear what it does to the sound it’s steering.

Two controls decide the effect. Rate is how fast it moves; depth is how far it pushes the thing it’s aimed at.

And it doesn’t have to be aimed at pitch. Point the same LFO at the filter cutoff instead and the note doesn’t wobble in pitch at all — it opens and closes, bright to dark, which is the wah and wobble you hear all over electronic music.

Try it

Open synth-lab.html#m05. Aim the LFO at pitch, set a slow rate and a small depth for vibrato, then wind the rate up until it stops sounding like a singer. Now switch the destination to filter cutoff, leave the settings alone, and listen to the same movement become a wobble.
